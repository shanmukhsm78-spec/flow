What this is

FLOW is being built so students and founders can think with AI without handing every thought to someone else’s servers.

Today that means a local prototype: chat through a model you run on your machine, with memory saved as a file you control. Ambition is larger. The product is not finished.

● Building toward

  • Privacy-first, on-device AI
  • Local chat + local memory
  • No cloud ownership of your chats by FLOW
  • Honest shipping, in public

○ Not claiming

  • A finished product
  • User counts or traction
  • Features that are not local yet
  • That a waitlist equals proof

Why it exists

I’m Shanmukh — a solo student founder in India. I’m building FLOW in public for people like me: students and founders who want useful AI without giving up the private parts of how they think.

The bet is simple. If the model and the memory stay on your device, the product can be useful without owning your conversations.

Global English. One person. One idea at a time.
